How we approach marketing · Resend Open main menu Features Company Enterprise Help Docs AI Pricing Log in Get started How we approach marketing Reading time 3min Updated Jul 8, 2026 Author When it comes to marketing, everything we do is about driving word-of-mouth growth. The idea is to create a machine that amplifies externally what is being built internally . On top of that, we want to be seen as experts in our field, so delivering genuinely useful tips to people is crucial. To do that, we use different communication formats and channels. Email We recognize that not all people use social media, and even when they do, the algorithm might not distribute our posts to them. That's why every month or so we try to send a newsletter to all of our users. This is typically a compilation of the most recent product updates including new changelogs, blog posts, customer stories, and open source launches. When we organize special events like launch weeks, we create unique audiences just for that specific event. When we do that, we can increase the frequency of messages such as daily emails during a launch week. Dogfooding is a huge part of our culture , so we use Resend Broadcasts for that. Video We invest in video because we believe video is a great medium to tell engaging stories , not because we want to maximize the number of views. Overall, we prefer having 120 views on a video that engages with the right audience over 20,000 views on a video that reaches random people who don't fit our ICP. Learn more about how we think about YouTube . Blog We haven't invested heavily in SEO yet. That's an area we want to explore, but we haven't been able to find a consistent framework or build a sustainable machine to approach this. Still, we try to publish something new on our blog at least once a month. These are typically company-related news like new hires or fundraising.

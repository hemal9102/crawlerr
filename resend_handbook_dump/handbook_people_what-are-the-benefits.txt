What are the benefits · Resend Open main menu Features Company Enterprise Help Docs AI Pricing Log in Get started What are the benefits Reading time 5min Updated Aug 26, 2026 Authors Remote Office Allowance (Global) Spend $2,000 to get your home office primed for optimal flow. This is in addition to the MacBook provided by Resend. Productivity Budget (Global) $500 per year for team members for tools and systems that make them more productive. No questions asked. Wellness Benefit (Global) Utilize $300 per month towards your wellness journey, split into two limits: $150 for mind wellness : therapy co-pays, meditation and mindfulness apps, wellness retreats, groups, or coaches, volunteering or creative class fees, and however you unplug (books, art materials, puzzles and board games, instruments, plants) $150 for body wellness : workout classes, memberships, fitness apps and equipment (wearables, weights), fitness challenges and event entrance fees, nutrition programs, supplements, or coaches, and services like massages or acupuncture These are just examples, so use it on whatever matters to you. The only exclusions are regular items, groceries, and routine aesthetic services (hair, nails, etc.). The limits are intentionally separate. Some of us are great at prioritizing the mind but neglect the body, and vice versa. This benefit is an incentive to explore both sides. It's also intentionally monthly and doesn't roll over. Both limits reset on the 1st. The goal is to build regular, ongoing wellness habits, not to save up for a big purchase. Purchases are paid via your Brex card. If a purchase goes over your limit, pay with your personal card and submit the eligible amount for reimbursement within 30 days. Personal Domain Registration (Global) Up to $100 to register a yourname.com domain if you don't already have one. We see writing as a powerful skill to master and we want to encourage our team to write more. Bi-Annual In-Person Offsite (Global) Travel and meet up with the entire team around the world. Unlimited Sick Days (Global) Take the time you need to rest, recover, and get back on track. 12 Weeks Family Leave (Global) Bond with your new biological or adopted child. This can be taken all at once or spread out over the first year. 20 PTO Days per Year (Global) Take time off for whatever reason you want. No work allowed. 10 Holidays per Year (Global) Choose the 10 US holidays or swap in local ones. 2026 Holiday Schedule: Jan 1: New Year’s Day May 25: Memorial Day Jun 19: Juneteenth Jul 4: Independence Day Sep 7: Labor Day Nov 26: Thanksgiving Nov 27: Black Friday Dec 24: Christmas Eve Dec 25: Christmas Dec 31: New Year’s Eve 401K (US) 401K account provided to invest in your future (no matching). Health, Dental, Vision (US) 100% Employee , 90% Family principal covered by Resend. Life Insurance (US) Care for your family and loved ones, even after a life crisis.

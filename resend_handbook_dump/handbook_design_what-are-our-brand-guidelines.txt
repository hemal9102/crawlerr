What are our brand guidelines · Resend Open main menu Features Company Enterprise Help Docs AI Pricing Log in Get started What are our brand guidelines Reading time 3min Updated Jul 8, 2026 Author Principles Simple Help navigate complexity and remove friction Modern Has a look and feel ahead of the present days Memorable A brand that stays and earns trust Name “Resend” is the brand name and always spelled with a capital “R”. It is a single word and should not be spelled as ”ReSend”, "resend", or any other variation. Symbol In July 2025, we unveiled a new visual identity , including a redesigned symbol that replaces the untreated "R" we used before. The symbol expresses three ideas in a single stroke: Friction-free : a smooth, uninterrupted path that mirrors the experience of using Resend Speed : a dynamic form that suggests movement and progress Intention : a mark inspired by the act of sending The wordmark combines the symbol with carefully designed letterforms, replacing the traditional "R". Typeface Here's an overview of the typefaces used in the Resend brand: Display: ABC Favorit by Dinamo Product: Inter by Rasmus Andersson Editorial: Domaine Display by Klim Type Foundry Colors Resend is dark mode first.
